Implement `spool::PrintSpool`, a fixed-slot print queue of job names. `queue` appends a job and throws `spool::SpoolFull` when every slot is taken. `release` removes and returns the job that has waited longest and throws `spool::SpoolEmpty` when nothing is waiting. `displace` appends a job, first dropping the longest-waiting job if every slot is taken. `abandon` clears the spool. `backlog()` reports how many jobs are waiting, `slots()` the configured capacity, and `full()`/`idle()` report the two boundary states. Zero slots throws `std::invalid_argument`. Jobs are released in arrival order, and the spool must keep working indefinitely as slots are reused.
