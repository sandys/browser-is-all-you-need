Implement `telemetry::SampleRing`, a fixed-depth telemetry ring of `int` samples. `push` appends a sample and throws `telemetry::RingFull` when the ring is saturated. `pop` removes and returns the oldest sample and throws `telemetry::RingEmpty` when drained. `force` appends a sample, first evicting the oldest if the ring is saturated. `purge` discards everything. `pending()` reports how many samples are held, `depth()` the configured depth, and `saturated()`/`drained()` report the two boundary states. A zero depth throws `std::invalid_argument`. Samples come back in the order they went in, and the ring must keep working indefinitely as indices wrap.
